Just Eat Apologises for AI Food Mistakes

BBC News・2026.10.21
AI ErrorDisclosure & Trust

UK delivery platform Just Eat tried using generative AI to create pictures for dishes that had no photo, and the AI misread regional menu names. Northern Ireland's "bacon and egg soda" (soda bread) became a glass of fizzy drink with bacon and eggs floating in it, a "chip muffin" turned into a sweet muffin stuck with fries, and a restaurant's misspelled "plane garlic bread" came out shaped like an airplane. Only after the images were mocked across social media did the company apologise, remove them and pause the feature. The aim had been to spare small restaurants the cost of photography, yet the price of the errors fell on those same restaurants' menus. Every image carried an AI label, which did nothing to stop the errors. The case is funny, but it makes two serious points: the AI failed at context, not drawing, taking literally a typo any human reader would quietly correct; and the people who caught the mistakes were customers, not the platform. Who should have looked at these images before they went live?
