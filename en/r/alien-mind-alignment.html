An Alien Mind

OpenAI・2026.09.06
Loss of ControlAI Agent

In this essay, OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki offers a central image: AI is grown, not designed. Deep learning systems are the product of one simple optimization step repeated countless times on enormous computing power; we can uncover local mechanisms but cannot understand the whole. Because these systems come into being so differently from humans, we cannot assume they will follow human principles. He acknowledges that alignment research may not keep pace with capability, and that chain-of-thought monitoring, the main tool for checking what AI is "thinking," is becoming less effective. He also warns that as AI gains autonomy, human misuse and AI's own misalignment will become harder to tell apart. He therefore argues that voluntary slowdowns should become normal, that safety thresholds should be enforced from outside the companies, and that governments should make international coordination a top priority. When the people building AI say they do not understand it well enough, how should users think about their own trust in it?
