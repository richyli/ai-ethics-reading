Anthropic Researchers Raise the Alarm as One Resigns

The New York Times・2026.09.10
Loss of Control

An Anthropic researcher who previously worked at OpenAI resigned publicly, saying neither company was acting responsibly: the leading labs are racing to build systems more capable than humans without adequate safeguards. A colleague working on alignment backed him, putting the risk of AI wiping out humanity within a decade above 10 percent and saying the company has no clear plan for aligning superintelligence. In July, more than 1,300 employees across the industry had signed an open letter asking the government to set rules that would slow development. The report also includes other views: the company says its safeguards are the strongest in the industry, while critics argue that AI firms exaggerate the danger to make their products look more valuable. What makes the piece worth reading is the race logic it exposes: others won't act responsibly, so I have to build it first. Every side can be sincerely worried and still keep accelerating. In a race like this, are humans still steering?
